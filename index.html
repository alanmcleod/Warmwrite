WarmWrite Quick Guide

Start writing
Create or open a document, tap the page and type.

Continue Writing
Close a file to return to the Home screen. Your latest document appears first.

Word count
Your total and session word counts appear below the page.

Save reminder
The page can gradually change from cream towards orange as unexported changes accumulate.

Focus Mode
Tap “Focus is Off” to hide the lower bars. The colour reminder remains active.

Open formats
WarmWrite opens and exports TXT and simple RTF documents.

Privacy
Your documents stay in this browser unless you export them.
